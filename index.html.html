                        🧬 The Bioscience Problem
                    

                    

                        To decrease reliance on expensive imported corn and soy, modern Malaysian poultry operations use localized alternative feeds. This model uses a standard cubic growth curve calibrated to match growth data from the Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) for Ross 308 broilers fed on oil palm-based rations over a standard 35-day cycle. 
                    

                    

                        Adapted baseline profile: 35-day cycle finishing at ~2.23 kg body weight with an optimized local Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR).
                    

                


                
                

                    
🧮 Mathematical Model

                    
                    

                        Growth Function (Cubic Fit)
                        

                            $$f(x) = -0.0001x^3 + 0.008x^2 + 0.015x + 0.043$$
                        

                        $f(x)$ represents accumulated Body Weight (kg) at day $x$. Initial chick weight ($x=0$) is $0.043 \text{ kg}$ (43g).
                    


                    

                        Growth Rate Function (Derivative)
                        

                            $$f'(x) = -0.0003x^2 + 0.016x + 0.015$$
                        

                        $f'(x)$ represents the instantaneous growth velocity ($\text{kg/day}$) at exactly day $x$.
